About Roshan Kirana Pasal

Roshan Kirana Pasal is a trusted local grocery shop located at Haranhiya Chauka–3, Bara, Nepal. Our shop has been serving the community with honesty, quality products, and friendly service for many years.

We believe that a kirana pasal is not just a place to buy goods, but a place of trust. From daily groceries to household essentials, we carefully select quality items so that every customer feels satisfied and valued.

👤 Our Founder

Prabhunath Prasad Yadav

Roshan Kirana Pasal was founded by Mr. Prabhunath Prasad Yadav, a hardworking and honest businessman who believes in service before profit.

With strong values, dedication, and deep respect for customers, he has built this shop into a reliable name in Haranhiya Chauka. His vision is simple yet powerful: “Provide good products at fair prices with a smile.”
